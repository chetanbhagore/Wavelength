## 🌟 The Philosophy: Anti-Metric Social Interaction

Modern social networks reduce human connection to transactional tallies: follower counts, like tallies, comment counts, and algorithmic outrage engines designed to maximize session duration.

**Wavelength reimagines social connection from first principles:**
Instead of broadcasting to an audience, users tune an analog radio dial to match their emotional frequency and enter a temporary 12-minute room with strangers who are on the exact same wavelength right now.

---

## 💎 10 Architectural & UX Innovations

### 1. 📻 Synthesized Mechanical Radio Detents (Web Audio API)
- **Zero Audio Assets**: Rather than loading heavy MP3/WAV files, Wavelength synthesizes tactile rotary clicks, white noise static bursts, and a 528Hz pure sine wave lock chime entirely in real time using the browser's `AudioContext`.
- **Detent Feedback**: Every step on the frequency dial triggers micro-oscillator impulses that mimic a vintage mechanical analog dial.

### 2. 🌊 The Living Ambient Canvas & Velocity-Reactive Fluid Motes
- **Dynamic HTML5 Canvas**: 42 floating stardust motes and a continuous sinusoidal waveform render at a fluid 60 FPS.
- **Velocity Reactivity**: Turning the dial accelerates stardust motes proportionally to the rotational angular velocity, bridging digital touch with physical momentum.

### 3. 👥 Multi-Agent Stochastic Audience Simulation (Ornstein-Uhlenbeck)
- **Brownian Drift Model**: Stranger presence is not hardcoded or linearly randomized. It follows an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck stochastic drift process with mean reversion, simulating natural human flocking behavior across frequencies.
- **Organic Cadence**: Strangers arrive, type with variable cadence (`stranger_XX is typing...`), pause to read, and resonate with messages without centralized server latency.

### 4. 💫 Room-Wide Collective Resonance Over Individual Likes
- **No Vanity Counters**: Clicking "Resonate" does not increment a "+1" badge next to your name.
- **Radial Light Wave**: Instead, it illuminates the entire space with an expanding radial light wave (`--color-accent-resonance: #33E6C9`), surging collective room energy and vibrating the collective atmosphere.

### 5. ⏳ The Sacred 30-Second Broadcast Dissolution Climax
- **Ephemeral Finality**: In the last 30 seconds of a 12-minute room, the interface enters a poetic dissolution phase.
- **Sensory Decay**: The UI progressively desaturates by up to 85%, CRT scanline jitter intensifies, and an audio lowpass biquad filter decays frequencies down to 120Hz as the broadcast signal dissolves into the void.

### 6. 📇 Physical 3D Gravity Echo Drop & Material Receipt Cards
- **Ritual of Departure**: Upon leaving or room expiry, participants drop an anonymous single-line reflection into the ether.
- **Physical Physics**: The reflection card drops with 3D perspective acceleration (`rotateX: 45deg, translateY: 140px`) accompanied by a synthesized sub-bass thud and 16 ascending ether spark particles.
- **Thermal Receipt Aesthetics**: Echoes render on the Echo Wall as material receipts with dashed perforation tear lines, unique station receipt codes (`#REC-XXXX`), and one-click retuning chips.

### 7. 👻 Dial Presence Residue (The Ghost Halo)
- **Anonymous Footprints**: When a user leaves a frequency, an ethereal breathing ghost halo lingers on that specific MHz dial detent (*"strangers were just here"*), preserving historical continuity without storing identities or personal data.

### 8. 🛡️ 100% Client-Side Privacy Architecture
- **Zero Tracking / Zero Identifiers**: No cookies, no local database logins, no device fingerprinting.
- **Ephemeral State Machine**: Room messages exist strictly in memory. Once the room dissolves, all conversational memory is permanently garbage-collected.

### 9. 📱 6-Tier Responsive Design Engine
- **Universal Device Coverage**: Meticulously crafted breakpoints spanning 320px (ultra-compact / foldables), 375px (iPhone SE), 480px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px+ ultra-wide.
- **Touch Targets**: Strictly enforces WCAG 2.5.5 touch targets (minimum 44×44px interactive areas) and `touch-action: manipulation` for zero mobile tap delay.

### 10. ♿ Inclusive Universal Accessibility
- **WCAG AA/AAA Contrast**: High-contrast typography on dark void themes.
- **Accessible Interactions**: Full keyboard rotary dial navigation (`ArrowLeft`, `ArrowRight`, `Home`, `End`), ARIA slider sematics (`role="slider"` with `aria-valuenow`), and complete `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` fallbacks.
